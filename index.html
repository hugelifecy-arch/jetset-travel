FAQ

Practical answers before you book.

Clear terms. Fast communication. No surprises.

Do you issue corporate invoices?

Yes. We issue corporate invoices with the details your finance team needs, including passenger and route references.

Can you split invoices by traveler, project, or cost center?

Yes. Tell us your structure in advance, and we format invoices for your internal workflow.

What happens if a flight changes or is cancelled?

We manage disruption handling: rebooking options, fare-rule checks, and clear next steps for each traveler.

Can you help with voluntary flight changes?

Absolutely. We explain penalties and fare differences first, then process the best available change option.

Do you provide visa support?

Yes, as an informational service: we guide documents and process steps. Final visa decisions are made only by consulates or migration authorities.

How fast do you respond?

Most requests get an initial response the same business day. Urgent in-trip issues are prioritized on WhatsApp.

Can you work within our corporate travel policy?

Yes. Share your policy rules and approval flow, and we propose options that match your compliance requirements.
