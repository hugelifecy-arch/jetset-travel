## Luxury Cruises from Cyprus in 2027: The Short Answer

*Checked October 2026.* The luxury cruise lines do not start their voyages in Cyprus. In 2027, Silversea, Regent Seven Seas Cruises, Seabourn and Explora Journeys start and end their Eastern Mediterranean voyages mainly in **Athens (Piraeus), Venice (Fusina), Istanbul, Trieste and Rome (Civitavecchia)**. If you live in Cyprus, you fly to the departure port, ideally the day before, and join the ship there.

On the cruise lines' own websites we could confirm two luxury calls at Limassol in 2027, both Regent: Seven Seas Navigator stays overnight on 21–22 January, and Seven Seas Mariner calls on 3 October. Both are stops on voyages between Istanbul and Athens, so you still board in Istanbul or Athens.

## What "All-Inclusive" Means on Each Line

Every luxury line calls itself inclusive, but the details differ:

- **Silversea:** butler service in every suite, drinks including champagne and selected wines, onboard gratuities, port taxes and Wi-Fi. Shore excursions are not in the basic fare.
- **Regent Seven Seas:** unlimited shore excursions, drinks, all speciality restaurants, Wi-Fi and prepaid gratuities. Concierge suites and higher also include one hotel night before the cruise.
- **Seabourn:** wines and spirits, all dining venues and Wi-Fi. Seabourn says tipping is "neither required nor expected". Shore excursions cost extra.
- **Explora Journeys:** nine dining experiences, drinks, Wi-Fi and onboard gratuities. A few speciality restaurants and wines cost extra, and shore excursions are not included.

If shore excursions matter to you, Regent is the only one of the four that includes them as standard.

## When to Fly In

The lines' advice differs:

- **Regent** strongly recommends arriving at least one night before the cruise.
- **Explora** accepts arrival on embarkation day for flights within Europe, if you allow at least 4 hours between landing and sailing (5 hours in Athens, Istanbul and Rome).

Our advice from Cyprus: fly in the day before. A delayed flight should not cost you the cruise, and for Istanbul, Trieste and Monte Carlo you need a connection anyway. We book the hotel near the port or in the city centre, whichever suits the embarkation time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do luxury cruises leave from Cyprus in 2027?

Not as far as we could find. Silversea, Regent, Seabourn and Explora start their Eastern Mediterranean voyages in Athens (Piraeus), Venice (Fusina), Istanbul, Trieste or Rome (Civitavecchia). Regent's ships call at Limassol on 21–22 January and 3 October 2027, but only as a stop.

### Which luxury cruise line includes shore excursions?

Of the four, only Regent Seven Seas includes unlimited shore excursions in the standard fare. Silversea offers an "All-Inclusive Plus" fare with a shore-excursion credit; Seabourn and Explora sell excursions separately.

### Can I fly from Paphos instead of Larnaca?

For Athens and Rome, yes: Ryanair flies direct from Paphos to both, and Aegean flies Paphos–Athens. For Venice and Barcelona you fly from Larnaca.

### How do I get to a cruise that starts in Istanbul?

There are no flights between the Republic of Cyprus and Turkey, so you fly via Athens or another hub. We usually suggest arriving the day before.

### Are flights included in luxury cruise fares?

Not by default on Silversea, Seabourn or Explora (Explora's "Sky & Sea" fare is the exception). Regent sells air through its own programme. Booking with us, you get flights from Larnaca or Paphos timed to the cruise, with a hotel night before if you need one.
