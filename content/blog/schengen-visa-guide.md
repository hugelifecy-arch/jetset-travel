For residents of Cyprus who hold non-EU passports, a Schengen visa is the gateway to 27 European countries. The application process is straightforward when you know what to expect, but small documentation errors can lead to delays or refusals. This comprehensive guide walks you through the entire process — from choosing the right consulate and gathering your documents to booking your appointment and tracking your application status.
