## The Mediterranean's Most Exceptional Destinations for 2026

The Mediterranean has always been synonymous with luxury travel, and 2026 is shaping up to be one of the most exciting seasons yet. New boutique hotel openings, expanded yacht charter routes, and a growing demand for authentic cultural experiences are redefining what luxury means in this part of the world — and many of the best destinations are just a short flight from Cyprus.

At JetSet Travel, we have spent nearly two decades curating bespoke Mediterranean itineraries for discerning travellers. As an IATA-accredited agency (Tourism Licence 7775), we have access to exclusive rates, preferred partnerships with luxury hotel groups, and the insider knowledge that comes from booking thousands of trips across this region. Here are the six destinations we are most excited about for 2026.

## 1. Santorini, Greece — Timeless Caldera Elegance

Santorini needs no introduction, but it continues to reinvent itself. The island's iconic caldera views, white-washed cave hotels, and world-class wine scene make it a perennial favourite — and 2026 brings new reasons to visit.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to June** and **September to October.** These shoulder months offer warm weather (25–30°C), fewer crowds than the July–August peak, and significantly better hotel availability and pricing. The sunsets are just as spectacular year-round.

### Getting There from Cyprus

Direct flights from Larnaca to Santorini (Thira, JTR) operate seasonally with Aegean Airlines and charter services. Flight time is approximately 1 hour 40 minutes. During off-season, connect through Athens (ATH) with a short 45-minute domestic hop.

### Where to Stay

**Canaves Oia Epitome** remains one of the finest addresses on the island — an adults-only retreat carved into the cliffs of Oia with infinity pools, private dining, and unobstructed caldera views. For a more intimate experience, **Mystique** (a Luxury Collection property) offers cave-pool suites with butler service.

### Why It Is Trending in 2026

Santorini's wine tourism has matured significantly, with guided vineyard tours, volcanic terroir tastings, and sommelier-led dinners becoming essential components of a luxury stay. The island is also investing in sustainable tourism infrastructure, making it an increasingly responsible luxury choice.

## 2. Amalfi Coast, Italy — La Dolce Vita Perfected

The [Amalfi Coast](https://www.italia.it/en/campania/amalfi-coast) remains the gold standard for Mediterranean glamour. The dramatic cliffside villages of Positano, Ravello, and Amalfi offer a combination of natural beauty, culinary excellence, and Italian style that is genuinely unmatched.

### Best Time to Visit

**Late April to June** and **September.** The Amalfi Coast is notoriously crowded in July and August, and the narrow coastal roads become congested. Spring and early autumn deliver warm temperatures, quieter beaches, and a more relaxed atmosphere.

### Getting There from Cyprus

Fly from Larnaca to Naples (NAP) — the nearest major airport — with connections through Athens or Rome. Flight time with one stop is approximately 3.5–4 hours. From Naples, a private transfer to Positano takes about 75 minutes, or you can arrive in style by private boat.

### Where to Stay

**Il San Pietro di Positano** is our top recommendation — a clifftop masterpiece with terraced gardens cascading down to a private beach, a Michelin-quality restaurant, and suites with floor-to-ceiling sea views. **Belmond Hotel Caruso** in Ravello offers an infinity pool that appears to float above the coast, with concert hall access and manicured Italian gardens.

### Why It Is Trending in 2026

New restrictions on tourist vehicles along the coastal road (SS163) are actually improving the experience for luxury travellers, who increasingly arrive by private boat or helicopter. Several new fine-dining restaurants and boutique wellness retreats have also opened along the coast.

## 3. Montenegro — The Adriatic's Best-Kept Luxury Secret

Montenegro has been building its luxury credentials quietly for years, and 2026 marks its arrival as a serious Mediterranean luxury destination. The combination of dramatic fjord-like coastline, medieval towns, and world-class resort development makes it one of the most exciting places to visit this year.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to September**, with **June and September** offering the best balance of warm weather and manageable crowds. The Bay of Kotor is particularly stunning in early summer when the surrounding mountains are still green.

### Getting There from Cyprus

Fly from Larnaca to Tivat (TIV) or Podgorica (TGD). Tivat is the more convenient airport for coastal resorts, located just minutes from Porto Montenegro and the Bay of Kotor. Connections typically route through Belgrade, Istanbul, or Athens.

### Where to Stay

**Aman Sveti Stefan** is the undisputed crown jewel — a 15th-century island village converted into an ultra-exclusive resort, connected to the mainland by a narrow causeway. With just 50 rooms and suites, it offers privacy, history, and Aman's signature understated luxury. **One&Only Portonovi** is a newer addition on the Bay of Kotor, offering a full-service spa, marina access, and panoramic views of the Adriatic.

### Why It Is Trending in 2026

Montenegro's EU accession progress is generating investment in tourism infrastructure while prices remain significantly lower than established rivals like Croatia and Italy. For luxury travellers, this means exceptional value without compromising on quality.

## 4. Sardinia, Italy — Emerald Coast Sophistication

Sardinia's Costa Smeralda has been synonymous with elite Mediterranean travel since the Aga Khan developed it in the 1960s, and it continues to attract the world's most discerning travellers. Beyond the famous emerald waters, Sardinia offers rugged interior landscapes, ancient archaeological sites, and some of Italy's finest cuisine.

### Best Time to Visit

**June and September** for the best combination of warm seas, sunshine, and relative tranquility. July and August are the peak months — Porto Cervo and the Costa Smeralda buzzes with yachts and high-profile visitors, which is either a draw or a deterrent depending on your preferences.

### Getting There from Cyprus

Fly from Larnaca to Olbia (OLB), the gateway to the Costa Smeralda. Connections typically route through Rome (FCO) or Milan (MXP). Total travel time is approximately 4–5 hours with one stop.

### Where to Stay

**Hotel Cala di Volpe** (a Marriott Luxury Collection property) is the iconic Costa Smeralda address — a Moorish-inspired resort on a private bay with a seawater pool, yacht marina, and legendary service. **Hotel Pitrizza**, its more intimate sister property, offers just 15 rooms with private gardens and direct sea access.

### Why It Is Trending in 2026

Sardinia is experiencing a wave of new yacht charter offerings, including expedition-style cruises around the Maddalena Archipelago. The island's food and wine scene — centred on pecorino cheese, bottarga, Vermentino wines, and fresh-caught seafood — is also gaining recognition from international food media.

## 5. Dubrovnik and Hvar, Croatia — Heritage Meets Coastal Glamour

Croatia's Dalmatian coast combines UNESCO World Heritage architecture with crystal-clear Adriatic waters, and the duo of Dubrovnik and Hvar offers the perfect luxury itinerary. Dubrovnik's medieval old town provides cultural depth, while Hvar delivers island sophistication with lavender fields, hidden coves, and sunset cocktails.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to June** and **September.** Dubrovnik in particular becomes extremely crowded in July and August due to cruise ship arrivals. For a more refined experience, visit during the shoulder months when the old town is atmospheric rather than overwhelming.

### Getting There from Cyprus

Fly from Larnaca to Dubrovnik (DBV) with connections through Athens, Zagreb, or Istanbul. Flight time with one stop is approximately 3–4 hours. From Dubrovnik, reach Hvar by catamaran (approximately 3.5 hours) or by private boat or helicopter for a more luxurious transfer.

### Where to Stay

**Villa Dubrovnik** — a boutique cliffside hotel just outside the old town walls with a private beach, sea-level pool, and views across the Adriatic to the Elaphiti Islands. On Hvar, **Maslina Resort** offers a design-forward Mediterranean retreat with organic gardens, a world-class spa, and sustainability at its core.

### Why It Is Trending in 2026

Croatia's growing focus on experiential luxury — wine trails along the Pelješac Peninsula, truffle hunting in Istria, and private island-hopping by sailboat — makes it far more than a beach destination. The Croatian kuna's replacement by the euro in 2023 has also simplified payments and pricing for European visitors.

## 6. Bodrum, Turkey — Where East Meets Mediterranean West

Bodrum has evolved from a backpacker favourite into one of the Mediterranean's most polished luxury destinations. The combination of ancient ruins (the Mausoleum of Halicarnassus), turquoise bays, and a booming high-end hotel and restaurant scene makes it an increasingly compelling choice — especially for travellers based in Cyprus.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to October**, with **June and September** offering the ideal combination of warm weather, warm seas, and calmer energy. Bodrum's summer club scene peaks in July and August, attracting a younger, livelier crowd.

### Getting There from Cyprus

Bodrum is one of the most accessible luxury destinations from Cyprus. Fly from Larnaca to Bodrum Milas Airport (BJV) with Turkish Airlines or Pegasus, often with a quick connection through Istanbul. Flight time is approximately 1.5–2.5 hours. Alternatively, seasonal charter flights operate direct.

### Where to Stay

**The Bodrum EDITION** offers Ian Schrager's signature design aesthetic on a private peninsula with three beaches, a clifftop pool, and a Turkish hammam. **Mandarin Oriental Bodrum** provides the chain's legendary service in a secluded cove setting with private beach residences and an extensive spa.

### Why It Is Trending in 2026

The favourable exchange rate between the euro and the Turkish lira means that luxury travel in Turkey offers exceptional value compared to equivalent experiences in Greece or Italy. New direct flight routes and expanded marina facilities are also making Bodrum increasingly easy to reach and explore by sea.
