## Six Luxury Mediterranean Escapes, and How to Reach Them from Cyprus

*Updated October 2026.* The Mediterranean has always been synonymous with luxury travel, and some of its finest places are close to Cyprus. Santorini is a direct summer flight from Larnaca; the others need one connection, and for each one we show you the simplest route.

JetSet Travel is an IATA-accredited travel agency based in Paphos (Tourism Licence 7775). We plan tailor-made trips from Cyprus, and for every destination below we explain how to actually get there from Larnaca or Paphos, because that is the part most travel guides leave out. Route information was checked in October 2026; schedules change, so we confirm current flights when you book.

## 1. Santorini, Greece — Timeless Caldera Elegance

Santorini needs no introduction. The island's caldera views, white-washed cave hotels and volcanic wines make it a perennial favourite.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to June** and **September to October.** These shoulder months offer warm weather (average highs around 23–28°C), fewer crowds than the July–August peak, and significantly better hotel availability and pricing. The sunsets are just as spectacular year-round.

### Getting There from Cyprus

In summer, Cyprus Airways flies direct from Larnaca to Santorini (Thira, JTR), roughly twice a week, with a flight time of about 1 hour 30 minutes. Outside that season there is no direct flight: fly Larnaca–Athens (about 1h35–2h, many daily flights with Aegean, Cyprus Airways, Sky Express and Wizz Air) and connect to Santorini (about 50 minutes).

### Where to Stay

**Canaves Epitome**, just outside Oia above Ammoudi Bay, has a private pool or plunge pool in every suite and villa and wide sunset views over the Aegean (it is not on the caldera side, and it welcomes families). For caldera views, **Mystique, a Luxury Collection Hotel**, sits on the cliff in Oia, with butler service and private plunge pools in its villas.

### Good to Know

Since July 2025 Greece has charged cruise passengers €20 a head to land on Santorini from June to September, part of an effort to manage day-trip crowds. Staying a few nights in the shoulder months is the calmer way to see the island.

## 2. Amalfi Coast, Italy — La Dolce Vita Perfected

The [Amalfi Coast](https://www.italia.it/en/campania/amalfi-coast) remains the gold standard for Mediterranean glamour. The dramatic cliffside villages of Positano, Ravello, and Amalfi offer a combination of natural beauty, culinary excellence, and Italian style that is genuinely unmatched.

### Best Time to Visit

**Late April to June** and **September.** The Amalfi Coast is notoriously crowded in July and August, and the narrow coastal roads become congested. Spring and early autumn deliver warm temperatures, quieter beaches, and a more relaxed atmosphere.

### Getting There from Cyprus

There is no flight from Larnaca to Naples. In season, Ryanair has flown direct from Paphos to Naples (about 2h55). Year-round, the simplest option is Larnaca–Rome Fiumicino (about 3h15–3h30; Wizz Air, Aegean in season, and Cyprus Airways from December 2026), then the high-speed train from Rome to Naples (about 1h10). From Naples, a private transfer to Positano takes around 1.5 hours, longer in summer traffic, or you can arrive by private boat.

### Where to Stay

**Il San Pietro di Positano** is our top recommendation: a clifftop hotel with terraced gardens running down to a private beach and a Michelin-starred restaurant, Zass. **Caruso, A Belmond Hotel** in Ravello occupies an 11th-century palazzo about 1,000 ft above the sea, with an infinity pool and terraced gardens.

### Good to Know

From spring to October, non-resident cars face odd/even number-plate restrictions on set days on the SS163 coast road. Taxis, licensed chauffeur cars and guests with a hotel booking are exempt, so a driver, taxi or boat is the easiest way to move between villages.

## 3. Montenegro — The Adriatic's Best-Kept Luxury Secret

Montenegro has been building its luxury credentials quietly for years. The combination of a dramatic fjord-like coastline, medieval towns and high-end resort development makes it one of the most interesting places on the Adriatic.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to September**, with **June and September** offering the best balance of warm weather and manageable crowds. The Bay of Kotor is particularly stunning in early summer when the surrounding mountains are still green.

### Getting There from Cyprus

There are no direct flights from Cyprus. The most practical route is via Belgrade: Larnaca–Belgrade with Air Serbia or Wizz Air (about 2h50, daily), then Air Serbia to Tivat (about 1 hour), the airport closest to Porto Montenegro and the Bay of Kotor. Aegean also flies Athens–Podgorica, with a reduced winter schedule.

### Where to Stay

**Aman Sveti Stefan** is the crown jewel: a 15th-century island village converted into a resort, connected to the mainland by a narrow causeway. It reopened in summer 2026 after a five-year closure (Villa Miločer and the beaches from late May, the island village from 1 July), and the island operates seasonally, so check dates. **One&Only Portonovi** is a newer addition on the Bay of Kotor, offering a full-service spa, marina access, and panoramic views of the Adriatic.

### Good to Know

Aman Sveti Stefan's reopening and Montenegro's advanced EU accession talks have put the coast firmly back on the luxury map.

## 4. Sardinia, Italy — Emerald Coast Sophistication

Sardinia's Costa Smeralda has been synonymous with elite Mediterranean travel since the Aga Khan and partners founded the Consorzio Costa Smeralda in 1962. Beyond the famous emerald waters, Sardinia offers rugged interior landscapes, ancient archaeological sites, and some of Italy's finest cuisine.

### Best Time to Visit

**June and September** for the best combination of warm seas, sunshine, and relative tranquility. July and August are the peak months — Porto Cervo and the Costa Smeralda buzzes with yachts and high-profile visitors, which is either a draw or a deterrent depending on your preferences.

### Getting There from Cyprus

There are no direct flights from Cyprus to Sardinia. Fly Larnaca–Rome Fiumicino (about 3h15–3h30), then on to Olbia, the Costa Smeralda airport (about 1 hour, several daily flights). In summer, Aegean also flies Athens–Olbia. Allow most of a day for the journey: flying time alone is about 4h20 before connection time.

### Where to Stay

**Hotel Cala di Volpe** (a Marriott Luxury Collection property) is the iconic Costa Smeralda address — a Moorish-inspired resort on its own bay with an Olympic-size saltwater pool. **Hotel Pitrizza**, its more intimate sister in Liscia di Vacca, has 66 rooms, suites and villas, a saltwater pool cut into the granite and a private beach.

### Good to Know

Beyond the beaches, Sardinian food and wine are a reason to go in themselves: pecorino, bottarga, Vermentino and fresh seafood.

## 5. Dubrovnik and Hvar, Croatia — Heritage Meets Coastal Glamour

Croatia's Dalmatian coast combines UNESCO World Heritage architecture with crystal-clear Adriatic waters, and the duo of Dubrovnik and Hvar offers the perfect luxury itinerary. Dubrovnik's medieval old town provides cultural depth, while Hvar delivers island sophistication with lavender fields, hidden coves, and sunset cocktails.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to June** and **September.** Dubrovnik in particular becomes extremely crowded in July and August due to cruise ship arrivals. For a more refined experience, visit during the shoulder months when the old town is atmospheric rather than overwhelming.

### Getting There from Cyprus

There are no direct flights from Cyprus. Two simple options: Paphos–Zagreb with Ryanair (about 3 hours), then Croatia Airlines to Dubrovnik (about 55 minutes), or, from spring to autumn, Larnaca–Athens and Aegean's seasonal Athens–Dubrovnik flight (about 1h35). From Dubrovnik, catamarans run to Hvar between April and October (about 3.5 hours); there is no winter sea connection, and a private boat is an option.

### Where to Stay

**Villa Dubrovnik** is a boutique cliffside hotel about a 20-minute walk from the Old Town, with a private rocky beach, an indoor-outdoor pool and views of the Old Town and Lokrum. On Hvar, **Maslina Resort** offers a design-forward Mediterranean retreat with organic gardens, a world-class spa, and sustainability at its core.

### Good to Know

Wine trails on the Pelješac Peninsula and island-hopping by boat make Dalmatia more than a beach destination. Croatia has used the euro since 1 January 2023, which keeps payments simple for visitors from Cyprus.

## 6. Bodrum, Turkey — Where East Meets Mediterranean West

Bodrum has evolved from a backpacker favourite into one of the Mediterranean's most polished luxury destinations. The combination of ancient ruins (the Mausoleum of Halicarnassus), turquoise bays, and a booming high-end hotel and restaurant scene makes it an increasingly compelling choice.

### Best Time to Visit

**May to October**, with **June and September** offering the ideal combination of warm weather, warm seas, and calmer energy. Bodrum's summer club scene peaks in July and August, attracting a younger, livelier crowd.

### Getting There from Cyprus

There are no flights between the Republic of Cyprus and Turkey, so Bodrum takes a little planning. The usual route from Cyprus is Larnaca–Athens (about 1h35–2h), then Athens–Kos (about 1 hour, Sky Express or Aegean), then the ferry from Kos to Bodrum (about 20–45 minutes; several sailings a day in summer, fewer in winter). You leave the Schengen area at Kos, so check Turkish entry requirements for your nationality before you travel.

### Where to Stay

**The Bodrum EDITION**, created by Ian Schrager with Marriott, sits on Yalıkavak bay with a sandy beach, beach club, infinity pool and a spa with a traditional hammam. **Mandarin Oriental Bodrum** provides the chain's legendary service in a secluded cove setting with private beach residences and an extensive spa.

### Good to Know

Bodrum's high season runs from June to September, and the sea is warm enough for swimming from May to October.
