## Going Home for the Winter

When the summer season ends in Cyprus, thousands of people who work here start planning the same trip: home to the Philippines, Nepal, Bangladesh or India for annual leave, Christmas, a family celebration or the gap between two contracts, and then back to Cyprus for the next season. This guide covers what you need to know for winter 2026/27: which airlines fly the routes, how special Labour and VFR fares work, when to book, and how to plan the return.

## The Short Answer

- **There are no direct flights** from Cyprus to Manila, Kathmandu, Dhaka or Delhi. Journeys start at **Larnaca (LCA)** and connect through a hub such as **Doha** (Qatar Airways), **Dubai** (Emirates) or **Bahrain** (Gulf Air). None of these three airlines flies from Paphos.
- **Special Labour and VFR fares** exist for people who travel for work or to visit family. They are sold through travel agencies, not on airline websites, and each airline sets its own rules.
- **A special fare is not always cheaper.** Always compare it with the best ordinary fare, including baggage.
- **Book as soon as your dates are known**, especially for Christmas and festival periods.
- **Plan the return to Cyprus at the same time**, and check that your residence or work permit is valid for the day you fly back.

If you already know where and when you are going, you can [request a Labour/VFR fare on WhatsApp](/en/labour-vfr-flights-cyprus) straight away.

## Who Travels Home from Cyprus in Winter

The numbers are significant. On 30 September 2025, according to figures from Cyprus's Deputy Ministry of Migration reported by the [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2025/11/17/legal-migrants-drive-growth-in-cyprus-says-minister), 15,607 Nepali and 14,237 Indian nationals held residence permits in Cyprus, along with 3,175 Bangladeshi nationals, and 6,106 Filipino nationals held domestic-work permits. Besides domestic work, many are employed in hotels, restaurants, care, agriculture and construction.

Seasonal hospitality staff tend to travel together: many hotels and restaurants wind down in late October and November and reopen in spring, so departures bunch up in the same weeks. That is one more reason to book early.

## Labour, VFR and Ordinary Fares: What Is the Difference?

Airlines sell the same seat under different fare types:

- **Labour fares** are filed for passengers travelling for work, typically people employed abroad who fly between their home country and the country where they work. Airlines can ask for proof of employment or residence.
- **VFR fares** (Visiting Friends and Relatives) are for people travelling to see family or friends.
- **Ethnic fares** is a trade term for discounted fares linked to the passenger's home country. They often overlap with Labour and VFR fares.
- **Published fares** are the ordinary fares anyone can buy, and **promotional fares** are time-limited sales open to everyone.

Labour and VFR fares are **private fares**: airlines distribute them only through authorised agencies, and their conditions (eligibility documents, baggage, change and refund rules) are not published on airline websites. That is why you ask an agency rather than search online. We explain the fare types in more detail on our [Labour & VFR flights page](/en/labour-vfr-flights-cyprus).

## Flights from Cyprus to Manila

Manila is the main destination for Filipino workers in Cyprus, and the busiest time is the run-up to Christmas. All three airlines fly the route with one connection: Larnaca to Doha with Qatar Airways, to Dubai with Emirates or to Bahrain with Gulf Air, then on to Manila.

For Manila we usually compare a special Labour or VFR fare from each airline with the best published fare, and look closely at the baggage allowance, because many travellers take gifts home for the holidays. If you know your return date, ask us to price the return to Cyprus together with the outbound. ## Flights from Cyprus to Kathmandu

For Kathmandu there are two main options: **Qatar Airways via Doha**, or **Emirates via Dubai**, where the Dubai–Kathmandu flight carries an Emirates flight number but is operated by its partner flydubai. Gulf Air does not currently fly to Kathmandu.

Nepali workers often travel home around Dashain and Tihar in the autumn, as well as at the end of the hotel season, so seats on the Kathmandu connections can fill early. Check the baggage allowance on both flights of the journey, as it can differ when two airlines operate the trip. [Request a Kathmandu fare](/en/labour-vfr-flights-cyprus#kathmandu).

Dhaka is served from Larnaca by all three airlines with one connection in Doha, Dubai or Bahrain. ## When to Book Winter Departures

Book **as soon as your dates are fixed**. Special fares have a limited number of seats on each flight, and the cheapest seats go first, especially around:

- **Christmas and New Year**, the peak for Manila and also busy for Delhi
- **Diwali and Tihar** (autumn), busy for Delhi and Kathmandu
- **Dashain** (autumn), busy for Kathmandu
- **Eid**, busy for Dhaka

Festival dates move each year, so check them when you plan. If your employer has not confirmed your last working day, ask us about a fare that allows date changes, and compare the change fee with the saving of a stricter fare.

## Planning the Return to Cyprus

Most people who work in Cyprus need to come back, so plan the return at the same time as the trip home:

- **Match your return to your contract or the hotel's reopening date**, and leave a margin in case a connection is delayed.
- **Check ticket validity.** A return ticket must be used within the period the fare allows.
- **Check your permit.** Your Cyprus residence or work permit should be valid on the day you fly back, or your renewal accepted for re-entry. Airlines can refuse boarding when the documents for the destination are not in order. If you are unsure, ask your employer or the Civil Registry and Migration Department before you leave.
- **Already at home?** We can also book a ticket to Cyprus that starts in Manila, Kathmandu, Dhaka or Delhi, where the airline's rules allow the fare to be sold that way.

## Baggage Considerations

Going home often means heavy bags, and baggage is where special fares differ most from ordinary tickets. Before you choose:

- **Check the checked baggage allowance** (pieces or total weight) on every flight of the journey, not just the first one.
- **Check cabin baggage** size and weight, which can be stricter than you expect.
- **Arrange extra baggage before the day of travel** if you need it. It is usually cheaper than paying at the airport.
- **Weigh your bags at home.** Overweight bags at check-in are the most common, and most expensive, surprise.

We never quote a baggage allowance from memory: we check it for your exact fare, because it can change between fare types and airlines.

## Airline Fare Conditions to Read Before You Pay

Every fare comes with rules. These are the ones we go through with you:

- **Date changes**: allowed or not, and the fee
- **Refunds and no-shows**: what you get back if you cannot travel
- **Validity**: the latest date the return can be used
- **Documents**: what the fare requires, such as your permit or an employer letter
- **Names**: the name on the ticket must match your passport exactly
