## What Corporate Travel Management Costs a Cyprus Company

*Updated October 2026.* There is no single price for managed corporate travel. What a Cyprus-based company pays depends on the pricing model, how many trips it books, how complex those trips are (multi-city itineraries, frequent changes, premium cabins), how much reporting and policy control it needs, and whether it wants support outside office hours.

This guide explains how agencies price corporate travel, what is usually included at each level, where the value tends to come from, and what to ask before you sign. We have left out fee ranges and savings estimates on purpose: they vary too much from one company to the next to be useful, so we quote for your company instead.

## How Corporate Travel Is Priced

Most agency pricing is one of three models, or a mix of them. Some agencies also earn commissions or incentives from airlines, hotels or other suppliers. Ask any agency whether it does, and whether those amounts are disclosed to you.

### Model 1: Transaction Fee per Booking

You pay a set fee each time the agency issues a ticket or books a hotel or car, usually with separate fees for changes and re-issues. Fees often differ by type of booking: a simple return flight within Europe takes less work than a multi-city itinerary or a complicated change.

- **Good for:** companies with steady, predictable booking volume, and finance teams that want every charge to match a line on an invoice.
- **Watch for:** the fee is the same whether a ticket is cheap or expensive, so on low-cost bookings it is a larger share of the total. Check what counts as a "transaction": for example, whether changes, cancellations or after-hours calls are charged separately.

### Model 2: Percentage of Spend

The fee is a percentage of what you spend on travel, usually billed monthly.

- **Good for:** companies with many lower-value bookings, where a fixed fee on every booking would add up.
- **Watch for:** the fee rises with your spend, including when fares go up or travellers book premium cabins, so the agency earns more when you spend more. If you choose this model, ask for a cap per booking or per year, or a regular review.

### Model 3: Flat Monthly Retainer

A fixed monthly fee for an agreed scope, which can include a set number of bookings, reporting and 24/7 disruption support.

- **Good for:** companies that want a predictable monthly cost and have a stable group of regular travellers.
- **Watch for:** paying for capacity you do not use in quiet months. Agree what is in scope, what happens above the agreed volume, and when the fee is reviewed.

### Combining Models

Models can also be combined: for example, a small retainer for support and reporting, plus transaction fees for the bookings you actually make. Whichever model you choose, ask for a written breakdown so you can compare agencies like for like.

## Where the Value Comes From

The agency fee is only part of the picture. Rather than promise a savings figure, here is where the value usually comes from and how to check it for your own company.

### 1. Ticketing and Fares

IATA accreditation allows an agency to sell and issue tickets for many airlines under a single agreement and to settle payments through IATA's Billing and Settlement Plan (BSP), according to [IATA](https://www.iata.org/en/services/travel-agency-program/accreditation-travel/). JetSet is IATA-accredited (IATA 14200130) and licensed by the Cyprus Deputy Ministry of Tourism (licence 7775).

Depending on its agreements with airlines, an agency may also be able to offer fares that are not sold on public websites. Whether any fare is cheaper than one you could find yourself depends on the route, the dates and the fare rules, so compare like with like: the same cabin, the same flexibility and the same baggage.

### 2. Hotel Benefits

Some agencies belong to hotel programmes or consortia that add benefits such as breakfast, room upgrades or late check-out at participating hotels. These vary by hotel and by programme, so ask any agency which programmes it belongs to and whether the hotels your team actually uses take part.

### 3. VAT Paperwork

Business travel can involve VAT in several countries, and the way a booking is invoiced decides what your company can reclaim. Points to check with your accountant (rules checked in October 2026):

- **VAT charged in other EU countries.** A Cyprus-registered business claims back VAT paid in another EU country, on hotels or car hire for example, through its own tax authority (the Cyprus Tax Department), not in the country where the VAT was charged ([European Commission](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/vat-refunds_en)). As of October 2026, under [Council Directive 2008/9/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32008L0009), the claim must be submitted by 30 September of the year after the one in which the VAT was charged, and whether the VAT can be refunded at all depends on the rules of the country that charged it.
- **Supplier invoices.** A refund claim lists each supplier invoice, with the supplier's VAT number and the VAT amount, so keep the hotel's or car-hire company's invoice and ask for it to be made out to your company.
- **Travel sold in the agency's own name.** When an agency sells travel in its own name using hotels and other services it buys in, the EU special VAT scheme for travel agents applies (Articles 306–310 of the [EU VAT Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32006L0112)). Under that scheme, the VAT those suppliers charged the agency cannot be deducted or refunded in any EU country. The scheme does not apply where the agency acts only as an intermediary. If reclaiming hotel VAT matters to you, agree at the start how hotels will be booked and invoiced.

### 4. Administrative Time

Booking, rebooking and chasing receipts takes staff time that rarely appears as a cost line. Before you compare quotes, estimate how many hours your team spends on travel admin each month and what that time is worth to you. That is the figure an agency fee should be compared with.

### 5. Disruption Support

When a flight is cancelled late on a Sunday evening, having someone who can rebook the trip and rearrange the hotel means your traveller is not left waiting on an airline's call centre. Ask any agency exactly what out-of-hours cover includes, who answers, and whether it costs extra.

## Two Example Profiles

These are illustrations to show how the models fit different companies, not client case studies.

### Example 1: A Growing Tech Company in Limassol

Many travellers, frequent short trips (mostly within Europe and to the UK), mostly economy, and plenty of last-minute changes. What matters most: fast changes, stored traveller profiles, a travel policy that is actually followed, and consolidated invoices for finance.

A transaction-fee model, or a hybrid with a small retainer for support and reporting, tends to suit this kind of company, because the cost follows the number of bookings.

### Example 2: A Small Financial Services Firm in Nicosia

A few senior travellers, fewer but more expensive trips (business class, premium hotels, some long-haul), complex itineraries, and a need for fast help at any hour.

A retainer with a named contact, combined with transaction fees rather than a percentage of spend, tends to suit this kind of firm, because a percentage fee grows with high fares while the amount of work per booking does not.

## When Managed Travel Is Not Worth It

We will tell you this honestly because it is better for you, and ultimately better for us to only sign clients who will stay.

Managed travel is not worth the retainer if:

- You travel only occasionally.
- Travel is all one destination, one traveller, one airline (for example, a single partner who flies only to London).
- You have no finance complexity: no VAT to reclaim, no policy to enforce, no reporting requirement.
- You have an existing relationship with an airline's direct corporate programme that already delivers most of the benefit.

In those cases, we happily quote per-booking transaction fees instead. You get ticketing help and disruption support without the retainer, and you use us when you need us.

## How to Run a Procurement Process

If you are moving from self-booking or switching agencies, keep the RFP tight. Five points are sufficient:

1. **Scope:** number of travellers, annual booking count, booking mix (flight/hotel/car), destinations.
2. **SLAs required:** response time, 24/7 cover, account manager availability.
3. **Reporting and invoicing:** report formats and frequency, integration with your expense system, and how hotels will be invoiced if you plan to reclaim VAT.
4. **Pricing model:** transaction fee, percentage, retainer or hybrid, with a written breakdown, plus whether the agency earns supplier commissions and how they are handled.
5. **Evaluation criteria:** IATA accreditation, Cyprus Deputy Ministry of Tourism licence, references from Cyprus companies of a similar size, disruption procedures in writing.

How long the switch takes depends on the number of travellers and how much profile and policy information needs to move. Ask for a written plan with dates covering profile migration, traveller onboarding, policy set-up and a period of running alongside your current process.

## FAQ

### How much does managed corporate travel cost in Cyprus in 2026?

It depends on the pricing model (transaction fee, percentage of spend, retainer or a mix), on how many bookings you make and how complex they are, and on the level of support and reporting you need. We do not publish fee ranges because they vary so much between companies. Tell us your travel profile and we will quote for your company.

### Do we need managed travel if we only travel occasionally?

Probably not on a retainer basis. A pay-as-you-go arrangement with an IATA-accredited agency can still be useful for ticketing, changes and help when trips are disrupted, and you only pay when you book.

### Can you handle our VAT invoicing?

Yes. We issue invoices that meet Cyprus VAT requirements, and we can consolidate them monthly or split them by department or cost code. If you plan to reclaim hotel or car-hire VAT charged in another EU country, tell us when you book, so that where the supplier allows it the invoice can be made out to your company. Your accountant should confirm what can be reclaimed.

### What about 24/7 support?

At the retainer tier, yes — a named contact with a mobile number, answered in under 30 minutes any time of day. At the pay-as-you-go tier, we cover office hours with an on-call rota for genuine emergencies.

### How long is a typical contract?

Contract length and notice period are set out in the proposal for your company. With any agency, check the notice period, any minimum term, and how your traveller profiles and booking records are handed over if you leave.
