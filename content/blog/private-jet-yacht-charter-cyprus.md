## Private Jet and Yacht Charter from Cyprus: The Short Answer

*Checked October 2026.*

- **Private jets:** Larnaca and Paphos are the Republic of Cyprus's two international airports, and both handle private flights. Larnaca has an executive terminal with passport control and customs inside. In Europe a charter flight must be run by an operator holding an Air Operator Certificate (AOC), and that is worth checking before you pay.
- **Yachts:** Limassol Marina and Ayia Napa Marina both take yachts over 100 metres. The classic day trips are the Akamas coast and Blue Lagoon from Latchi, the Limassol coast, and the sea caves of Cape Greco. For a week on board, most people fly to Greece, Croatia, Montenegro, Italy or the French Riviera, because the nearest Greek island of any size, Rhodes, is 255 nautical miles from Limassol.

## Flying Private from Cyprus

### Which airports

- **Larnaca and Paphos** are the only legal points of entry to the Republic of Cyprus by air. Both are run by Hermes Airports and operate 24 hours.
- **Larnaca** has an executive terminal for private flights, run by Skylink Services, with a passenger lounge and with customs, immigration and security inside the building. We confirm the handling arrangements for each flight.
- **Paphos** handles private flights too. We found no dedicated private-jet terminal there; ground handlers meet the aircraft and help passengers through customs and immigration.
- **Airports in the north of the island** are not legal entry points to the Republic; arriving that way counts as illegal entry. Private aircraft, like airlines, do not fly directly between the Republic of Cyprus and Turkey.

### Passport control

As of October 2026, Cyprus is not yet part of the Schengen area. It expects to join in 2027, subject to a decision of the EU Council. Until then, every passenger arriving in Cyprus goes through passport control, including on private flights from Schengen countries. EU, EEA and Swiss citizens can use a national ID card; everyone else needs a passport, and some nationalities need a visa. See our [Cyprus Schengen guide](/en/schengen-cyprus-2026) for the latest.

### Charter rules: why the AOC matters

Under EU rules, commercial passenger flights, including private jet charters, may only be operated by a company holding an Air Operator Certificate and an operating licence. The European Business Aviation Association warns that illegal or "grey" charters, flown without them, can leave passengers without valid insurance cover, including their own life insurance. Its advice is to ask for the operator's name and AOC number and check them with the aviation authority that issued it.

### Day charters around Cyprus

- **Akamas and the Blue Lagoon, from Latchi:** the Blue Lagoon lies west of Latchi on the Akamas peninsula, about 30–45 minutes by boat. Akamas is a protected Natura 2000 area, so boats should keep their anchors off the seagrass.
- **Lara Bay is off-limits in summer:** from 1 May to 31 October boats may not sail or anchor in waters up to 20 metres deep at Lara–Toxeftra, to protect nesting turtles. Any Akamas itinerary we plan in those months leaves Lara out.
- **The Limassol coast, from Limassol Marina:** Amathus is 5 nautical miles away, Governor's Beach 12, Kourion 18, Pissouri Bay 22 and Aphrodite's Rock 28.
- **Cape Greco and Ayia Napa:** sea caves that are best seen from the water, and Konnos Bay, which is sheltered from the prevailing wind by the Cape Greco peninsula.

### When to go

Cyprus has a long, dry summer from mid-May to mid-October. Cyprus Tourism puts the open-sea temperature above 22°C from June to November, reaching about 27°C in August. In summer the wind is usually an afternoon sea breeze from the west or south-west, often above 15 knots in May–July, with calmer mornings. Morning departures make for the smoothest day at sea.

### Charter licensing

Companies renting out yachts without crew (bareboat charter) need an attestation from the Shipping Deputy Ministry, and each vessel must be inspected and insured. A new Recreational Navigation Law (Law 4(I)/2026) has been passed, but as of August 2026 it was not yet in force. Whoever you book with, ask whether the company and the boat are licensed.

## Week-Long Yacht Charters: Why Most Start in Greece

Limassol Marina gives the sea distances: Rhodes is 255 nautical miles from Limassol, Kastelorizo 205 and Heraklion in Crete 395. That is a long passage before a holiday starts, so most week-long charters begin at a base closer to the islands. From Cyprus you fly there and step on board:

- **Greece:** Athens (Alimos Marina) and Lavrion for the Saronic Gulf and the Cyclades; Lefkada and Corfu (Gouvia Marina) for the Ionian; Kos and Rhodes for the Dodecanese. In the Cyclades the summer meltemi, from June to September, can reach 30 knots, so we match the area to your group.
- **Croatia:** Split, Trogir and Dubrovnik.
- **Montenegro:** Porto Montenegro in Tivat, with berths for yachts up to 250 m and customs and immigration on site.
- **Italy:** Naples, Procida, Castellammare di Stabia and Salerno for the Amalfi Coast and the islands.
- **French Riviera:** Antibes, Cannes, Nice and Monaco.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I fly private from Paphos airport?

Yes. Paphos handles private flights through ground handlers, who meet the aircraft and help with customs and immigration. Larnaca has the dedicated executive terminal.

### Do I go through passport control on a private flight to Cyprus?

Yes. Cyprus is not yet in the Schengen area, so every arriving passenger goes through passport control, including on private flights from Schengen countries. At Larnaca this happens inside the executive terminal.

### How do I know a private jet charter is legal?

Ask for the operator's name and its Air Operator Certificate number, and check them with the aviation authority that issued the certificate. Flights without an AOC can leave you without valid insurance.

### Where is the best day trip by yacht in Cyprus?

The Akamas coast and Blue Lagoon from Latchi is the classic choice, with the Limassol coast and Cape Greco as good alternatives. From May to October, Lara Bay is closed to boats in waters up to 20 metres deep to protect turtles.

### Can I charter a yacht in Cyprus for a week?

Yes, but the nearest Greek islands are a long way off (Rhodes is 255 nautical miles from Limassol), so most week-long charters start in Greece, Croatia, Montenegro, Italy or France. A week around Cyprus itself is possible for those who want it.
