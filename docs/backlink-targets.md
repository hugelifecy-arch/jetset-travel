## Anchor-text discipline

- Vary anchors — do NOT send all 30 with identical text. Target distribution:
  - Brand match (e.g. "JetSet Travel") — ~40%
  - Branded + service (e.g. "JetSet Travel — visa services Cyprus") — ~30%
  - Descriptive topical (e.g. "IATA-accredited travel agency in Paphos") — ~20%
  - Naked URL — ~10%
